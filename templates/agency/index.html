Good questions

Before we
begin.

Still curious? Ask us anything.

What kind of projects are a good fit?

We are at our best when a meaningful decision needs a clearer point of view: a new offer, a change in direction, a digital experience, or a team that needs momentum.

How do engagements usually start?

Most start with a short working session. We map the decision, the people it affects, and the smallest useful next step before we talk scope.

Do you work with in-house teams?

Yes. We often embed alongside brand, product, and marketing teams. Our job is to add perspective and capability, not create a parallel universe.

Where are you based?

Our collective works across New York, London, and wherever the work needs us. We use a mix of focused in-person sessions and thoughtful remote collaboration.
