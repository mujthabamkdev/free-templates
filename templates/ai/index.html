Ask us anything ↗
What does an engagement start with?+

Usually, a 45-minute working conversation. We map the moment that feels heavier than it should, then agree on what evidence would make a first step worthwhile.

Do you build custom AI products or use existing tools?+

Both. We choose the smallest reliable system that solves the problem—sometimes that is a connected workflow, sometimes it is a new product surface, and often it is a thoughtful combination.

How do you handle sensitive data?+

We begin with data boundaries and access needs, not model selection. Every build includes a review of permissions, retention, human oversight, and what should never enter a model context.

Can our team maintain what you build?+

That is the point. We document decisions, pair with your people, and leave behind clear operating habits so the system becomes a capability—not a black box.
