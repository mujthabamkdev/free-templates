Clear by design.

These starter pages provide a clear place to publish reviewed policy and accessibility information. Replace sample content with approved legal copy before launch.

Privacy

This template does not collect or store personal data by itself. The consultation form is demo-only and prevents network submission. When connecting a backend, document collection purpose, lawful basis, retention, processor details, user rights, and a privacy contact.

Terms

Replace this section with terms governing your services, project scope, intellectual property, payment, acceptable use, confidentiality, warranties, and termination. Have counsel review final text.

Accessibility

Kiteworks aims to support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, semantic headings, and responsive layouts. Test final content and integrations with assistive technology before publishing.

  • Keep interactive labels, validation errors, and status updates descriptive.
  • Provide an accessible route for reporting an issue and requesting an accommodation.
  • Review third-party embeds and authentication flows for equivalent access.
