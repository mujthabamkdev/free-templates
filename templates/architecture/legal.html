03 · Accessibility

Accessibility commitment

We aim to make this site usable with keyboard navigation, zoom, reduced motion, screen readers, and a broad range of devices. The template uses semantic headings and landmarks, a skip link, labelled fields, native validation, visible focus indicators, descriptive labels for decorative visuals, and responsive layout.

Before publishing, test final content with keyboard-only navigation, VoiceOver or another screen reader, 200% zoom, high contrast settings, touch targets, color contrast tools, and reduced-motion preferences. Provide a monitored accessibility contact and a clear way to report barriers.
