Before you plug in.

Ask a question ↗
Can I take an EV home for a test drive?

Yes. Bring your license and insurance; most drives take 30–45 minutes. We can also arrange a longer drive when your schedule allows.

How do you check battery health?

Our technicians run a diagnostic report, review charging history where available, and include the current state of health on every certified listing.

Do you offer home charging help?

We can recommend compatible Level 2 equipment and trusted local installers. Ask your guide for an at-home charging plan during your visit.

Are prices really all-in?

Listed prices are starting prices before government incentives, taxes, title, registration, and lender fees. We show every applicable fee before you decide.
