These starter sections give your dealership a place to publish reviewed privacy, terms, pricing, and accessibility information. Replace sample content with your approved policy copy before launch.

Privacy

This template does not collect or store personal information by itself. The demo inquiry and trade forms are intercepted locally when JavaScript is enabled. Before launch, connect forms to a reviewed service, explain the information collected, document retention and rights, and publish a privacy contact.

Terms, pricing, and vehicle information

Vehicle descriptions, pricing, range, availability, reviews, and finance examples on this template are illustrative. Replace them with verified dealership data. Publish terms covering vehicle purchases, deposits, test drives, trade-ins, financing, incentives, warranties, service, cancellations, and dispute handling. Have counsel review final copy.

Accessibility

Voltline aims to support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, semantic landmarks, responsive layouts, and descriptive form status updates. Test final content and vendor integrations with assistive technology before publishing.

  • Provide an accessible phone, email, or form route for reporting an accessibility barrier.
  • Keep labels, validation errors, success messages, and vehicle information descriptive.
  • Review financing, scheduling, chat, map, and inventory integrations for equivalent access.
  • Maintain a current accessibility statement and target response timeframe.
