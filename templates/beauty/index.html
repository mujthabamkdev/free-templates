How do I choose a service?

Tell us what you have now, what you want to change, your timing, and any relevant hair or scalp history. We can recommend a starting point during a consultation.

Can I bring reference photos?

Yes. References help us discuss shape, finish, color direction, and maintenance. We will also talk about how a reference translates to your hair and routine.

Is a patch test required?

Some color and chemical services may require a patch test or strand test in advance. Follow your salon’s written instructions and tell your artist about allergies or prior reactions.

What is your cancellation policy?

Publish your real policy here. This illustrative studio asks guests to provide 48 hours’ notice; late changes or missed appointments may incur a fee after confirmation of terms.

Does this booking form make a real appointment?

No. This template form is a demo and does not send or store information. A real studio needs a secure booking service, confirmation flow, privacy notice, and clear cancellation terms.
