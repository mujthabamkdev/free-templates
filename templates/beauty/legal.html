Privacy & demo forms

This template does not provide a privacy policy or a production booking service. The demo request form is intentionally disabled until JavaScript enables a local-only demonstration; its submit handler prevents transmission and storage.

Before launch, explain what information you collect, why you collect it, retention, processors, access controls, user rights, cookies, analytics, and how someone can contact your privacy owner. Do not request health details, payment data, or private images through an unreviewed form.

Accessibility statement

Build on this page with your real accessibility commitment, support contact, known exceptions, and remediation process. This template includes a skip link, semantic landmarks, visible focus styles, keyboard-friendly navigation, responsive reflow, reduced-motion support, labels, and descriptive text for decorative artwork.

Test with keyboard-only navigation, zoom and reflow, screen readers, contrast tools, touch targets, and current browsers. Keep service information and booking alternatives available without requiring a pointer or a particular assistive technology.

Safety, sensitivities & patch tests

Service suitability varies. Ask guests to share allergies, sensitivities, prior reactions, medications or treatments that are relevant to a service through a secure confirmed channel. Follow manufacturer and professional instructions for patch tests and strand tests; never promise a result.

Publish an appropriate route for urgent reactions or medical questions. A salon website is not emergency care and should not diagnose symptoms.
