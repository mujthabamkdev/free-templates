Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Northline Build Co. aims to make this website usable by as many people as possible. The template includes semantic landmarks, a skip link, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus indicators, descriptive labels, live form status, responsive layout, and reduced-motion support.

Test your final site with keyboard-only navigation, zoom to 200%, a screen reader, touch targets, color contrast tools, and current browsers. Add a contact method for reporting barriers and document the standard you target (for example, WCAG 2.2 AA) after review.
