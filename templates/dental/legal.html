01

Privacy & demo form

The appointment form on this template is a front-end demo. With JavaScript enabled, it prevents submission, displays a confirmation note, and resets fields. With JavaScript disabled, it remains clearly labeled as unavailable. No entry is transmitted, stored, or echoed by this template.

Do not add patient names, diagnoses, detailed symptoms, medical records, insurance numbers, or other sensitive information to the demo. Before launch, connect a privacy-reviewed scheduling channel, publish a current privacy notice, define retention and access controls, and confirm any required consent language.

02

Accessibility statement

This template uses semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus indicators, keyboard-operable navigation, labelled form controls, native disclosure panels, reduced-motion styles, and responsive layouts. A production practice should test the complete site with keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom, contrast tools, touch targets, and representative assistive technology.

Contact details, accommodation processes, translation support, and service information must be completed by the real practice and kept current.

03

Health disclaimer

Information in this template is general, illustrative, and educational. It is not medical or dental advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for a conversation with a qualified professional. Availability, fees, insurance participation, outcomes, and suitability vary.

This website is not monitored for emergencies. For severe pain, swelling, bleeding, injury, breathing difficulty, or another urgent concern, contact local emergency services or an appropriate urgent-care resource.

04

Before publishing

Replace names, credentials, phone numbers, addresses, hours, services, review language, legal copy, structured data, canonical URLs, and social metadata. Obtain permissions for patient stories, images, reviews, and outcome examples. Have clinical, legal, privacy, and accessibility reviewers approve the final content.
