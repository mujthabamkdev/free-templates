The six-week arc

A rhythm for
real progress.

Each week makes the next decision easier. Sessions are illustrative and should be adapted to the actual teaching team and learner needs.

  1. 01

    Notice the useful question

    Find the question underneath your idea and name who it might serve.

    Live workshop · 90 min
  2. 02

    Listen before you build

    Practice lightweight research and turn assumptions into conversations.

    Practice lab · 60 min
  3. 03

    Shape a small promise

    Define the smallest useful version of your project and how to test it.

    Peer pod · 45 min
  4. 04

    Make the first version

    Build a clear prototype, draft, or experience people can respond to.

    Studio week · self-directed
  5. 05

    Learn from the response

    Read feedback with curiosity, then decide what to keep, change, or release.

    Critique circle · 75 min
  6. 06

    Tell the story forward

    Share what you made, what you learned, and the next honest step.

    Showcase · 90 min

Who is this cohort for?

This illustrative cohort is designed for people with a promising project or question who want structure, feedback, and a supportive group. Define your real audience and prerequisites before launch.

How much time should I set aside?

Plan for the live workshop plus roughly two to three hours of practice each week. The actual workload should be stated clearly in your enrollment materials.

Are sessions recorded?

Set a clear recording, captioning, and retention policy for your real program. This template does not host sessions or store recordings.

What if I need accessibility support?

Tell the teaching team what would make participation workable, without sharing sensitive information in this demo form. Confirm captions, materials, schedule, and communication access before enrollment.

Can my employer pay?

Many learning budgets require an invoice or learning outcomes summary. Confirm whether your real program can provide those documents and what payment terms apply.
