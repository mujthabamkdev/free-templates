Accessibility

This template includes a skip link, semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, keyboard-operable navigation, reduced-motion support, responsive reflow, labelled form fields, and descriptive labels for inline illustrations. Confirm actual captioning, transcripts, platform accessibility, materials, time-zone support, communication access, and accommodations with the teaching team. Publish only details you can maintain.

Privacy & demo forms

The enrollment form on this template is a demo. JavaScript prevents submission and does not send, store, or echo values. With JavaScript disabled, browser form submission navigates back to the section and may place entered values in the URL; do not enter sensitive information. Before launch, connect forms to a privacy-reviewed service with clear consent, retention, access, and deletion practices.

Learning claims disclaimer

Brightpath content is general educational information, not legal, financial, career, medical, or mental-health advice. Learning outcomes depend on context, participation, prior experience, access, and support. Do not promise employment, income, credentials, promotion, transformation, or other outcomes unless claims are accurate, substantiated, and reviewed. Learners should seek appropriate professional advice for personal decisions.
