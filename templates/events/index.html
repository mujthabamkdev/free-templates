Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Have a question that is not here? Send an inquiry.

Where do you plan events?

We are based in the Hudson Valley and consider destination work selectively. Share your location and date; we will let you know if the fit and travel scope make sense.

How far in advance should we reach out?

Many clients reach out 9–18 months ahead for weddings and 2–6 months ahead for smaller gatherings. Availability varies; an inquiry starts a conversation, not a commitment.

Do you work with our existing vendors?

Yes. We can collaborate with your team and recommend partners when you need them. Any vendor availability, pricing, and performance should be confirmed directly in writing.

What happens after I inquire?

We review your date and notes, then reply with next steps if the scope looks like a possible fit. The demo form below does not send or store information until connected to a reviewed backend.

Can you accommodate accessibility needs?

Yes, we welcome an early conversation about mobility, sensory, dietary, communication, and other access needs. We will coordinate with the venue and vendors, while final accessibility conditions must be verified on site.
