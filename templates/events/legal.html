Template policy pages

Clear is kind.

These starter sections provide a readable home for privacy, terms, accessibility, and cancellation information. All copy is illustrative and must be replaced and reviewed before publishing.

Privacy

This template does not collect or store personal data by itself. The inquiry form is demo-only until connected to a backend. When you connect forms, analytics, mailing lists, payment tools, or scheduling services, explain what you collect, why, where it is stored, how long you retain it, and how people can exercise their rights.

Publish a real privacy contact, cookie or analytics disclosure where required, and the names of processors used by your business.

Terms

Replace this section with the terms governing proposals, contracts, deposits, travel, vendor relationships, event-day scope, intellectual property, cancellations, rescheduling, and website use. Include current contact details and have counsel review final copy for your jurisdiction.

Cancellations & rescheduling

State your actual deposit, payment schedule, cancellation windows, rescheduling process, force majeure terms, vendor fees, and refund rules in the signed agreement. Website package prices and availability are illustrative and are not offers or guarantees.

Accessibility

Gather & Glow aims to support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, semantic headings, descriptive labels, and responsive layouts. The template includes a skip link, mobile-menu focus handling, native form validation, and reduced-motion styles.

  • Tell visitors how to report a website or event accessibility barrier.
  • Discuss mobility, sensory, dietary, communication, and other access needs early.
  • Verify final venue routes, restrooms, seating, lighting, sound, parking, and vendor practices in person.
  • Provide an equivalent way to contact you if any embedded service is inaccessible.
  • Test final copy and third-party tools with assistive technology before launch.
