Questions, answered

Before you
walk in.

Still unsure? Send a practical question and the studio can help with next steps.

Do I need experience?

No. Tell the coach where you are starting, and ask for options whenever you need them. Class descriptions here are illustrative; confirm suitability with the real studio.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, water, and a way to arrive a few minutes early. Ask about footwear, equipment, and changing facilities before your first visit.

Can I pause or modify a movement?

Yes. Pausing and choosing an alternative are normal parts of a coached session. Let your coach know what support would make the class workable.

What if I have an injury or health concern?

Speak with an appropriate healthcare professional about your situation and tell the studio what you need to discuss before joining. Coaches do not diagnose or replace medical advice.

How do I cancel?

Cancellation windows and membership terms vary. Confirm current terms with the studio before booking; this template does not process reservations.
