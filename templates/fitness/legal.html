Accessibility

This template includes a skip link, semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, keyboard-operable navigation, reduced-motion support, responsive reflow, labelled form fields, and descriptive labels for inline illustrations. Confirm actual building access, equipment, communication support, and accommodations with your studio team and publish only details you can maintain.

Privacy & demo forms

The first-class form on this template is a demo. JavaScript prevents submission; it does not send, store, or echo values. Before launch, connect forms to a privacy-reviewed service with clear consent, retention, access, and deletion practices. Do not request sensitive health information through an unprotected form.

Movement and health disclaimer

Northline content is general fitness information, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Individual responses vary. Stop activity that causes pain, dizziness, or unusual discomfort and seek appropriate professional guidance for personal health questions. The studio should review all safety language with qualified advisers.
