Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I know which service to choose?

Search by a plain-language keyword, then read each service description and eligibility guidance. If you are unsure, contact the help team.

Can I get help in another language?

Yes. Interpretation and translated materials can be requested at no cost. Tell the service team what language you prefer.

What should I bring to an appointment?

Bring your confirmation, a current photo ID when required, and the documents listed on your service page. Requirements vary by service.
