Accessibility

We aim to make this website usable by everyone, including people who use assistive technology, keyboard navigation, captions, magnification, or other accommodations. This template follows semantic HTML, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences.

If you encounter a barrier, contact the service team and describe the page, task, device, and assistive technology involved. Provide a dedicated accessibility email, phone number, response target, and formal complaint process before production.

Language access

Public information should be available in the languages used by your community. Provide translated priority content and free interpretation for service interactions. Add a language selector only after translations, routing, and content governance are ready; do not use an unlabeled machine-translation widget as the sole option.

Privacy

This demonstration site does not send, store, or process personal information. The service request form is a non-functional preview. In production, publish a reviewed privacy notice that explains collection, purpose, retention, access, sharing, security, cookies, and contact details.

Connect forms only to approved systems, minimize data collection, provide clear consent where required, and avoid putting sensitive information in URLs or analytics events.
