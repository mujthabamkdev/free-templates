Our rooms are calm and characterful, our pantry is always open, and the water is never far away.

01

Morning

Walk the breakwater before breakfast. Look for seals near the red marker, then follow the smell of cinnamon back to the harbour.

20 min walk · easy

02

Afternoon

Borrow a bike and take Shore Road north. The little farm stand at mile five has the best wild blueberry hand pies around.

2 hr round trip · gentle

03

Evening

Bring a sweater to Lantern Beach. At low tide the rocks make a natural seat for watching the sun slip behind the island.

15 min drive · tide dependent
