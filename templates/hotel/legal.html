Starter copy for a fictional hotel template. Replace with reviewed, property-specific notices before launch.

01 · Privacy

Privacy, clearly.

This template has no analytics, trackers, cookies, external assets, or backend. The demo forms do not transmit or store submissions.

Before launch, explain what personal data the real property collects, why it is used, retention periods, service providers, international transfers, cookie choices, and how guests can submit privacy requests.

Replace this starter with a reviewed privacy notice and a real privacy contact.

02 · Booking & demo

A preview, not a reservation.

This site is an illustrative template. Room names, rates, availability, reviews, contact details, policies, and destination information are placeholders.

No booking, payment, confirmation, or message is processed here. Connect the forms to a reviewed booking or inquiry service before publishing, and clearly disclose final prices, taxes, fees, availability, cancellation rules, and the booking provider.

03 · Accessibility

A welcome for everyone.

The template uses semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus states, keyboard-friendly controls, descriptive labels, and reduced-motion support.

For a real property, publish verified details about step-free routes, lifts, room and bathroom layouts, service animals, parking, communication support, and how to request an accommodation. Provide a monitored contact for access questions and report any known barriers.

04 · Terms

Set expectations plainly.

This starter page is not legal advice. Before launch, publish terms reviewed for the property and booking channel, including reservation and payment conditions, cancellation and no-show rules, taxes and fees, occupancy, age and identification requirements, pets, conduct, liability, accessibility commitments, governing law, and contact details.

Terms shown during checkout should match the final confirmation and any third-party provider terms.
