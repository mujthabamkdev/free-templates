Before we
begin.

A first conversation is a chance to understand what you need and whether we are the right fit.

What happens in an initial consultation?

We listen to your situation, identify immediate priorities, and explain possible next steps. An initial conversation does not create an attorney-client relationship.

How do you handle conflicts?

We run a conflicts check before accepting any engagement. Please share only high-level information until we confirm that we can speak with you.

Do you take matters outside your listed practices?

Sometimes. Our connected network of trusted counsel helps us find the right support when a matter reaches beyond our core practices.

Is information submitted through this form confidential?

No. Please do not send confidential or time-sensitive information through this website. We will reply with a secure way to continue the conversation.
