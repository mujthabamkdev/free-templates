01 · Privacy & confidentiality

Protect what
people share.

Starter notice: Describe what personal information your firm collects, why it is collected, the lawful basis where applicable, retention periods, service providers, transfers, security measures, individual rights, and the approved contact method for privacy requests.

Add your actual intake, conflicts, confidentiality, and incident-response processes before launch.

02 · Consultation demo

A safe
starting point.

The consultation form on this template is a front-end demonstration only.

What the demo does

The form does not send, transmit, store, or review submissions. Its local status message is illustrative and is not an invitation to disclose a legal matter.

Before connecting an endpoint

Replace the demo behavior only after selecting an approved intake system, adding server-side validation and spam protection, documenting consent and retention, securing transport, and completing a privacy and conflicts review.

What visitors should provide

Ask only for the minimum high-level information needed to route an inquiry. Tell visitors not to include confidential, privileged, or time-sensitive details, and provide a secure next step after conflicts are checked.

03 · Terms

Information,
not representation.

This website provides general information about a fictional law firm template. It is not legal advice, a legal opinion, an offer of representation, or a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction.

Using this website, contacting the firm, or submitting an inquiry does not create an attorney-client relationship. Representation begins only after an approved engagement process, conflicts review, and written agreement. Do not rely on website content for a deadline or legal decision.

Publish reviewed terms covering acceptable use, intellectual property, content accuracy, external links, jurisdiction, governing law, limitation of liability, and any professional-conduct disclosures that apply to your firm.

04 · Accessibility

Open to
every visitor.

We aim to make this site usable with keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom, contrast settings, touch devices, and reduced-motion preferences. This template uses semantic landmarks, a skip link, labelled controls, visible focus states, responsive layout, and native form controls.

Before publishing, test the final content with keyboard-only navigation, VoiceOver or another screen reader, 200% zoom, contrast tools, touch targets, and reduced-motion settings. Provide a monitored accessibility contact, response target, and barrier-reporting process here.

05 · Illustrative content

Examples are
not promises.

Review every claim before a real visitor sees it.

Civic & Alder, its lawyers, offices, addresses, credentials, results, timelines, testimonials, client descriptions, and other content in this template are fictional or illustrative placeholders. They do not describe actual clients, matters, outcomes, awards, rankings, or future results.

Replace sample content with verified information and obtain any required permissions, substantiation, ethics review, and approval before publication. Results depend on the facts, law, venue, and strategy of each matter and are not representative of every outcome.
