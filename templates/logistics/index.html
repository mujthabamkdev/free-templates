Questions before the quote

Start with
what you know.

A thoughtful first conversation can start with partial information. We will identify what still needs confirming.

What information helps you scope a shipment?

Origin, destination, ready date, delivery window, commodity, dimensions, weight, access notes, and any documentation or service requirements you already know. Estimates remain subject to confirmation.

Do you own trucks or warehouses?

Northstar is presented here as a coordination partner. This template does not represent a specific owned fleet, warehouse network, carrier authority, or service territory. Confirm operating model and subcontractor relationships during discovery.

Can you support cross-border freight?

Cross-border coordination is an example service in this template. Actual customs support, carrier coverage, brokerage responsibilities, and documentation requirements depend on your shipment and should be confirmed before booking.

How does shipment tracking work?

The tracking view above is a front-end demonstration. A production deployment should connect to approved carrier, TMS, or customer data with access controls, retention rules, and a clear source-of-truth policy.

Do you guarantee delivery dates?

No. Transit estimates and appointments depend on carrier capacity, traffic, weather, border conditions, facility constraints, and other variables. Confirm the latest plan and service terms for each move.
