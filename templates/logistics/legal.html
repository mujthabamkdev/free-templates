Accessibility statement

This template aims to provide a strong baseline: semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible keyboard focus, labeled controls, readable contrast, responsive layout, reduced-motion support, and a keyboard-operable mobile navigation menu.

Before launch, test the final content and integrations with keyboard-only navigation, zoom and reflow, screen readers, contrast tools, form validation, touch targets, reduced motion, and current browsers. Keep headings, labels, error messages, status updates, and focus movement meaningful as content changes.
