01 · Privacy

Handle information
with care.

This template does not operate a real practice or collect personal information. Its consultation form is a front-end demo: JavaScript prevents submission, and no values are sent, stored, or echoed. Do not enter health information, photos, medication details, insurance numbers, or other sensitive data.

A production site should publish a current privacy notice describing its legal basis, data categories, processors, retention, access rights, security practices, cookies or analytics, contact route, and any jurisdiction-specific obligations. Connect forms only to a privacy-reviewed, secure service with appropriate access controls.

02 · Accessibility

Design for more
ways of moving.

This template includes a skip link, semantic landmarks, keyboard-visible focus, responsive navigation, form labels, descriptive SVG alternatives, and reduced-motion support. A real practice should test with keyboard navigation, zoom and reflow, screen readers, contrast tools, touch targets, and assistive technology before launch.

Offer an accessible contact route and describe how visitors can request communication or access accommodations. Keep content readable, avoid relying on color alone, and ensure third-party scheduling tools meet your accessibility requirements.

03 · Health disclaimer

Information is not
personal medical advice.

Website content is general information only. It cannot diagnose, treat, prevent, or assess a condition, and it does not replace an examination or conversation with a qualified professional. Treatment suitability, risks, side effects, alternatives, costs, and expected outcomes vary by person.

Do not use this template for urgent care. For severe pain, swelling, bleeding, injury, breathing difficulty, vision changes, signs of infection, or other urgent concerns, contact local emergency services or an appropriate urgent-care resource. A real practice should replace this guidance with locally reviewed instructions.

Never imply guaranteed results, universal suitability, or a specific outcome. Real before-and-after imagery and testimonials require documented permission, truthful context, and clinical, legal, and regulatory review.
