01 · Privacy

Handle health information
with care.

This standalone template has no backend, analytics, trackers, cookies, or external assets. The appointment form is a front-end demonstration; its demo script prevents submission and does not transmit, store, or echo entered values.

Do not enter diagnoses, detailed symptoms, medication lists, medical records, insurance numbers, dates of birth, photos, or other health or sensitive personal information into the demo. Health information can be specially protected under applicable law. Before launch, publish a reviewed privacy notice, define the purpose and retention of each data field, document access controls and processors, and confirm required notices, consents, and patient rights for your jurisdictions.

02 · Appointment request

A demo request is
not a booking.

The request form on the home page is for interface preview only. It does not schedule an appointment, confirm availability, create a patient record, establish a clinician–patient relationship, or promise a response. With JavaScript enabled, it shows a demo-only status message and resets the fields.

Replace the demo behavior with a privacy-reviewed scheduling or intake service before collecting real information. Publish confirmation timing, cancellation rules, communication preferences, security practices, and the correct consent language for that service.

03 · Emergency care

Not monitored for
urgent concerns.

This website and its demo form are not monitored for urgent or emergency requests and cannot assess symptoms or provide medical advice. Do not wait for a website response if you may be experiencing a medical emergency.

For an emergency, contact local emergency services. For an urgent but non-emergency concern, use an appropriate local urgent-care resource or the confirmed clinical contact for the real practice. Replace this general guidance with reviewed local instructions, hours, and crisis resources before launch.

04 · Terms

Use this page as
a starting point.

This template and its content are provided for demonstration and customization. Information is general, may be incomplete or out of date, and is not medical, legal, insurance, or other professional advice. No warranty, availability promise, treatment outcome, or service relationship is created by viewing or using this template.

Before publishing a real clinic site, replace names, claims, contact details, service descriptions, policies, and legal notices; obtain permissions for content and testimonials; and have qualified clinical, privacy, accessibility, and legal reviewers approve the finished site. Your published terms should address the real practice, jurisdiction, services, communications, intellectual property, and limitations of liability.

05 · Accessibility

Make room for
more ways to access care.

This template includes semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, native disclosure panels, descriptive text for illustrations, responsive layout, and reduced-motion support. These features are a starting point, not a conformance claim.

A real clinic should test final content and workflows with keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom and reflow, contrast tools, touch targets, and representative assistive technology. Publish a verified accessibility contact, accommodation process, language support, and access details, and provide a clear way to report barriers.

06 · Illustrative content

Every detail shown
needs review.

Morrow Clinic is a fictional template identity. Names, roles, portraits, quotes, services, credentials, address, phone number, hours, availability, insurance language, outcomes, and patient experiences are illustrative placeholders. They do not represent a real clinic, clinician, patient, endorsement, or verified result.

Replace or remove every placeholder and verify each remaining claim before launch. Do not publish an illustrative testimonial, clinical credential, location, emergency instruction, service claim, or patient story as if it were real.
