01

Privacy & demo form

The inquiry form on this template is a front-end demo. With JavaScript enabled, it prevents submission, displays a confirmation note, and resets fields. With JavaScript disabled, the form has no configured endpoint and cannot transmit information. No entry is transmitted, stored, or echoed by this template.

Do not add names, diagnoses, detailed symptoms, medication information, records, insurance numbers, or other sensitive information to the demo. Before launch, connect a privacy-reviewed intake or scheduling channel, publish a current privacy notice, define retention and access controls, and confirm any required consent language.

02

Accessibility statement

This template uses semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus indicators, keyboard-operable navigation, labelled form controls, native disclosure panels, reduced-motion styles, and responsive layouts. A production practice should test the complete site with keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom, contrast tools, touch targets, and representative assistive technology.

Replace this note with the practice’s current accessibility contact, accommodation process, language support, service information, and any applicable legal statement. Avoid claiming conformance without testing.

03

Health & crisis disclaimer

This website is for general information and template demonstration. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, medical advice, or a clinician–client relationship. Illustrative specialties, team details, testimonials, locations, fees, hours, and availability must be verified before use.

The website and demo form are not monitored for urgent messages and cannot provide crisis support. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the U.S., call or text 988; elsewhere, contact an appropriate local crisis resource. Replace this guidance with current, locally reviewed resources.
