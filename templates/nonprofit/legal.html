01

Privacy by explanation

The forms on this template are front-end demos. JavaScript prevents submission, displays a confirmation note, and resets the fields. With JavaScript disabled, each form points back to this page without a configured data endpoint. No entry is transmitted, stored, or echoed by this template.

Do not enter financial account details, health information, legal matters, safeguarding reports, passwords, or other sensitive information. Before launch, publish a current privacy notice, define purposes and retention, document access controls, and connect only reviewed vendors.

02

Accessibility statement

This template includes semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus indicators, keyboard-operable navigation, labelled controls, native disclosure panels, reduced-motion styles, and responsive layouts.

A production organization should test the complete service with keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom and reflow, contrast tools, touch targets, captions, language support, and representative assistive technology. Add a real accessibility contact, accommodation process, and response commitment. Do not claim conformance without testing.

03

Forms, giving & stories

The newsletter and volunteer forms are demo-only and are not monitored. The donation section is a handoff placeholder; it does not collect payment. Replace both with accessible, privacy-reviewed services, consent language, support contacts, receipt information, and an operational owner.

All names, partner marks, events, metrics, reports, stories, and impact language on the home page are illustrative. Replace composites with permissioned, verified content and give people a way to correct or withdraw their story.

04

Contact & urgent needs

This template does not provide emergency, crisis, legal, financial, or safeguarding support. Add your organization’s current contact details, response hours, reporting pathways, and local emergency guidance before publishing. For urgent danger, contact local emergency services.
