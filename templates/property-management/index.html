Good to know

Questions,
answered plainly.

Use this section to set expectations before a first conversation. Update all policies and response times for your operation.

What types of properties do you manage?

This illustrative site describes apartments, townhomes, single-family homes, and small communities. A real management company should state its property types, minimums, and service boundaries here.

Do you offer leasing-only support?

Leasing support can be scoped separately from ongoing management. Ask about marketing, inquiry handling, showings, screening, and the handoff process before signing an agreement.

How are maintenance requests handled?

Residents should use the approved portal or contact route in their lease materials. Urgent situations should follow the emergency instructions for the property; this demo page is not monitored.

How do owners receive updates?

Reporting cadence and contents depend on the agreement. Discuss financial reporting, maintenance records, inspections, and escalation preferences during onboarding.

Can I ask about a property outside these areas?

Send a general inquiry with the property location and type. Coverage, travel, and vendor availability should be confirmed before any relationship begins.
