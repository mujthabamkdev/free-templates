Privacy information

This standalone template does not include analytics, advertising pixels, external fonts, third-party embeds, or a backend. The example forms use a local JavaScript preview and do not send or store entries. Without JavaScript, each form falls back to its in-page anchor and still does not provide a submission service.

When you adapt this site, document what personal information you collect, why you collect it, how long you keep it, who processes it, and how people can exercise their rights. Link to a reviewed privacy notice from every relevant form and explain any cookies or third-party services you add.

Demo forms

Owner inquiries and maintenance requests are intentionally illustrative. Do not ask people to submit passwords, payment information, lease documents, emergency details, or other sensitive information through this template. Connect forms to a secure, monitored system and replace the demo success message before publishing.

Accessibility statement

This template uses semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus styles, labelled controls, native disclosure elements, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, and a keyboard-operable mobile menu. CSS-generated artwork is decorative or paired with a text alternative.

Test your final content and integrations with keyboard navigation, zoom and reflow, color contrast, screen readers, mobile browsers, and any resident portal you link. Provide a monitored contact route for accessibility feedback and state the standard and target you use.
