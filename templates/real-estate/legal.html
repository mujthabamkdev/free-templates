A useful starting point

Legal &
accessibility.

This starter page gives your property website a clear place for privacy, terms, accessibility, and contact guidance. Replace its placeholders with reviewed, property-specific copy before launch.

01 · Privacy

Privacy,
clearly.

This standalone template has no analytics, trackers, cookies, external assets, or backend. The demo inquiry form does not transmit or store submissions.

Before launch, identify the data controller, explain what inquiry data you collect, why you use it, lawful bases, retention, processors, international transfers, user rights, cookie choices, and how to make a privacy request.

Cookie preferences: publish a working consent control if the production site uses non-essential cookies. Do not describe a preference center that does not exist.

02 · Inquiry demo

A preview,
not a lead.

The inquiry form is a local demonstration only. It does not send email, create a viewing request, reserve a home, take payment, or create a customer record.

Before publishing, connect it to a reviewed service, document the service provider and retention period, secure the submission flow, and update the consent and confirmation language to match what actually happens.

03 · Property notice

Illustrative
by design.

Property names, addresses, illustrations, floor areas, prices, ratings, resident stories, team details, status labels, and availability shown on this template are illustrative demo content.

They are not an offer, valuation, reservation, guarantee, or statement of current availability. Before launch, verify every property detail and show the effective date, tenure, taxes, fees, service charges, specification, and any material restrictions required for the market where the site operates.

Prices and availability can change without notice. Direct visitors to an accountable sales contact for current information and ensure marketing claims can be substantiated.

04 · Terms

Set expectations
plainly.

This starter page is not legal advice. Before launch, publish terms reviewed for the property business, jurisdiction, sales process, and any third-party services.

Cover inquiry and viewing arrangements, reservation and payment conditions, cancellation, taxes and fees, tenure, eligibility, age and identification requirements, conduct, liability, intellectual property, accessibility commitments, governing law, and how to contact the responsible business.

Terms shown at reservation or checkout must match the final confirmation and any provider terms presented to the customer.

05 · Accessibility

A welcome
for everyone.

This template uses semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus states, keyboard-friendly controls, descriptive labels, live status messages, responsive layout, and reduced-motion support.

Test the final site with keyboard-only navigation, zoom to 200%, screen readers, touch targets, color contrast tools, and current browsers. Publish the accessibility standard you target, such as WCAG 2.2 AA, after review.

Provide a monitored route for reporting barriers and requesting an accommodation. For a real development, add verified details about step-free routes, lifts, entrances, parking, accessible homes, communication support, and service animals.

06 · Contact guidance

Make it easy
to reach you.

Publish the legal business name, registered or service address, monitored email address, telephone number, operating hours, and a clear contact for privacy and accessibility questions.

Current template contact details are placeholders: hello@atelierrow.example and +44 (0)20 5550 1408. Replace them before launch and confirm that every channel is monitored.

For urgent property, safety, or transaction questions, direct visitors to the responsible local team or professional adviser rather than relying on this demo page.
