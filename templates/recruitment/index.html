Our approach

Less matching.
More understanding.

We take time to understand the human shape of a role: the context, the ambition, and the conditions where someone can do their best work.

How we work ↗
  1. 01

    Listen properly

    We ask the questions behind the brief, then make space for honest answers.

  2. 02

    Make it clear

    We share the details people need to make an informed decision—not a polished half-truth.

  3. 03

    Connect with intent

    We introduce people because the fit is real, not because a quota needs filling.

  4. 04

    Stay alongside

    We keep listening through offer, notice period, and the first important weeks.

Good questions

Before we
begin.

Still curious? Start a conversation.

Do candidates pay to use Harbor & Hart?

No. Candidates never pay for our recruitment service. Our employer partnerships fund the work.

Do you only work on advertised roles?

No. Some of our best conversations start before a role is public. Share what you’re looking for and we’ll be clear about what may fit.

What happens after I get in touch?

We reply with a clear next step. That might be a conversation, a request for more context, or an honest note that we’re not the right partner today.

How do you handle candidate data?

This template’s forms are demo-only and do not collect or transmit information. In production, publish your retention, consent, access, and deletion practices before collecting candidate data.
