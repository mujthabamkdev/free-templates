01 / Privacy

Privacy notice

This sample notice explains the kind of information a real Harbor & Hart site might collect. It is placeholder content, not legal advice. Before launch, name the data controller, lawful bases, processors, retention periods, international transfers, rights, and contact route that apply to your organisation.

Information we may receive

A production site may receive contact details, CVs, work history, preferences, and correspondence when a person chooses to share them. Keep collection limited to what is needed for a stated recruitment purpose.

Your choices

Explain how people can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or withdrawal of consent. Provide a monitored privacy contact and a response timeframe.

02 / Terms

Website terms

Content on this sample website is illustrative. Vacancy titles, companies, compensation, statistics, testimonials, and outcomes are placeholders and do not constitute offers, guarantees, or employment advice.

Use of any production site should be governed by reviewed terms covering intellectual property, acceptable use, third-party links, service limitations, and the jurisdiction that applies.

03 / Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We aim to make this site usable with keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom, high contrast, and reduced-motion settings. This template includes a skip link, labelled controls, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, and a responsive menu with Escape support.

Tell people how to report a barrier, include a monitored contact route, and publish the accessibility standard and review date used by your organisation.

04 / Candidate data

Important demo notice

The candidate and employer forms on this template are front-end demonstrations. JavaScript prevents submission and no data is sent or stored. Without JavaScript, the forms use a same-page GET fallback and still do not connect to a recruitment system.

Before enabling a real endpoint, add server-side validation, encryption in transit, access controls, retention and deletion workflows, consent language, spam protection, audit logging, and a reviewed privacy notice. Never ask for sensitive personal data in an unprotected form.
