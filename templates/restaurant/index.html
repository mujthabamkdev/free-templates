Our story

A little room for big-hearted food.

We opened Juniper & Hearth with a borrowed oven, a stack of handwritten recipes, and a belief that a neighborhood restaurant should feel like an exhale.

Today, our small team cooks from the market, makes almost everything in-house, and knows regulars by their usual order. Come as you are; we’ll keep a light on.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Send us a note.

Do you take reservations?

Yes. Reserve online below for parties of up to eight. For larger groups, choose “Private dining” and share a few details.

Do you have vegetarian or gluten-aware options?

We always offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-aware dishes. Menus change often; please tell your server about allergies so we can guide you safely.

Can I bring my dog?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome on our covered patio. Service animals are welcome throughout the restaurant.

Do you host private events?

We do. Our back room seats up to thirty guests for dinners, celebrations, and workshops. Send an inquiry and our team will follow up.
