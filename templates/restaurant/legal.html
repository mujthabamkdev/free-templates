Welcome, clearly.

These starter sections provide a readable home for your restaurant’s reviewed privacy, terms, and accessibility information. All copy below is illustrative and must be replaced before publishing.

Privacy

This template does not collect or store personal data by itself. The reservation form is demo-only until you connect a backend. When you connect a reservation, mailing-list, ordering, analytics, or payment service, explain what you collect, why you collect it, where it is stored, how long you retain it, and how guests can exercise their rights.

Publish a real privacy contact, cookie/analytics disclosure where required, and the names of processors used by your restaurant.

Terms

Replace this section with the terms that govern reservations, cancellations, deposits, private events, gift cards, ordering, accessibility requests, and website use. Include current contact details and have counsel review the final copy for your jurisdiction.

Accessibility

Juniper & Hearth aims to support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, semantic headings, descriptive labels, and responsive layouts. The template includes a skip link, mobile-menu focus handling, keyboard-operable menu tabs, native form validation, and reduced-motion styles.

  • Tell guests how to report a website or in-person accessibility barrier.
  • Keep hours, menus, policies, and reservation errors available in plain language.
  • Test final copy, third-party ordering/reservation tools, maps, and payment flows with assistive technology.
  • Provide an equivalent way to reserve or contact the restaurant if any embedded service is inaccessible.
