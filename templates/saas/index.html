What is Northstar?

Northstar is a collaborative workspace that helps growing teams connect goals, projects, and the people behind them. It gives everyone the context to make progress with confidence.

How long is the free trial?

Every plan starts with a full 14-day trial. No credit card is required, and you can invite your whole team to explore Northstar together.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your workspace settings at any time. Changes are reflected on your next billing cycle.

Is my team’s data secure?

Northstar uses industry-standard encryption and access controls to keep your workspace protected. Our Scale plan includes additional security reviews and controls.
