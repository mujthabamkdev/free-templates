Template policy pages

Clear by design.

These starter pages give your team a clear place to publish reviewed policy and accessibility information. Replace all sample content with your approved legal copy before launch.

Privacy

This template does not collect or store personal data by itself. Connect the trial form to a privacy-reviewed service, document the purpose of collection, and publish your retention, rights, and contact details here.

Terms

Replace this section with the terms that govern your product, trial, billing, acceptable use, support, warranties, and termination. Have counsel review the final text.

Accessibility

Northstar aims to support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, semantic headings, and responsive layouts. Test your final content and integrations with assistive technology before publishing.

  • Report an accessibility issue through your published support channel.
  • Keep interactive labels, error messages, and status updates descriptive.
  • Review third-party embeds and authentication flows for equivalent access.
