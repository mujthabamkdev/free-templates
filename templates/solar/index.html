Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Start with a roof review, not a hard sell. There is no obligation to use this demo form.

Will solar eliminate my electric bill?

Not necessarily. Production, rates, credits, fixed charges, seasonality, system size, and export rules affect a real bill. Do not promise elimination without verified modeling and required disclosures.

Do I need a battery?

It depends on outage priorities, utility rates, export rules, available space, budget, and how you use energy. A qualified designer should compare options using clear assumptions.

How long does installation take?

Schedules vary with design, permitting, equipment, inspection, utility interconnection, weather, and local workload. Publish a range only after reviewing your actual process.

Can solar work on my roof?

Roof orientation, shade, structural condition, age, electrical service, and local rules all matter. A site assessment is needed; this template cannot determine suitability.

Are these projects and reviews real?

No. Names, photos, system sizes, results, ratings, reviews, credentials, service areas, and contact details are illustrative demo content until replaced and verified.
