Northline Journeys · template notes

Clear words.
Good practice.

This page is a starting point for the legal, booking, privacy, and accessibility content a real tour operator needs. It is not legal advice. Replace illustrative language with reviewed, jurisdiction-specific policies before launch.

01 · Booking & price disclosure

No booking happens here.

This standalone template does not take reservations, accept payment, check live availability, or create a traveller record. Journey names, itineraries, group sizes, dates, rates, taxes, inclusions, guide profiles, reviews, and partner details are illustrative placeholders.

Before publishing, state the legal operator, seller of record, currency, taxes, mandatory fees, deposit and balance schedule, cancellation terms, insurance expectations, accessibility information, minimum group size, supplier terms, and what happens if a departure changes. Show a final total before payment.

02 · Privacy

Privacy notice placeholder.

This template has no analytics, cookies, trackers, external assets, backend, or network requests. The inquiry form is intercepted in the browser and displays a local demo message only; it does not transmit or store content.

Replace this with a reviewed notice describing personal data collected, purpose, lawful basis, retention, processors, international transfers, user rights, security, cookies, marketing preferences, and a monitored privacy contact. Update form behaviour and consent language when connecting a real CRM or booking provider.

03 · Terms

Terms placeholder.

Publish reviewed terms for site use, intellectual property, external links, itinerary changes, supplier relationships, deposits, cancellations, force majeure, traveller responsibilities, insurance, complaints, and governing law.

Do not present illustrative rates, claims, testimonials, route descriptions, sustainability statements, safety information, or availability as verified facts. Ensure production copy matches contracts and current supplier information.

04 · Accessibility

Make room for everyone.

This template aims to support keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom, reduced motion, and a range of devices through semantic landmarks, a skip link, labelled controls, native validation, visible focus indicators, responsive layouts, and descriptive text for illustrative visuals.

Before launch, test the finished site with keyboard-only navigation, VoiceOver or another screen reader, 200% zoom, high contrast, touch targets, colour contrast tools, and reduced-motion settings. Publish verified access details for every route, accommodation, transfer, activity, rest stop, and dietary process. Offer a monitored accessibility contact and explain how barriers are reported.

Illustrative contact: access@example.com. Replace this address and response commitment with your own.

05 · Content & assets

A self-contained starting point.

This website uses plain HTML, CSS shapes, and inline SVG visuals. It has no external fonts, images, libraries, trackers, or hosted services. Replace the visual system only with approved local assets, meaningful alternative text, responsive crops, performance budgets, and confirmed usage rights.
