01

Privacy & demo form

The appointment form on this template is a front-end demo. With JavaScript enabled, it prevents submission, displays a confirmation note, and resets fields. With JavaScript disabled, fields remain disabled and a clear notice explains that nothing is sent or stored.

Do not add pet names, diagnoses, detailed symptoms, medical records, microchip numbers, insurance details, or other sensitive information to the demo. Before launch, connect a privacy-reviewed scheduling channel, publish a current privacy notice, define retention and access controls, and confirm required consent language.

02

Accessibility statement

This template uses semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus indicators, keyboard-operable navigation, labelled controls, native disclosure panels, reduced-motion styles, inline SVG alternatives, and responsive layouts. A production practice should test the complete site with keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom, contrast tools, touch targets, and representative assistive technology.

Publish real accommodation details, translation support, accessible contact methods, and a process for reporting barriers. Keep those details current.

03

Veterinary disclaimer

Information on this template is general, illustrative, and educational. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or guarantee an outcome for any animal, and it does not replace advice from a licensed veterinarian who can examine the pet and review its history.

This website is not monitored for urgent messages and is not an emergency service. For breathing difficulty, collapse, uncontrolled bleeding, suspected poisoning, severe trauma, or another urgent concern, contact local emergency services or an appropriate emergency veterinary hospital immediately.

04

Contact & content review

Replace illustrative contact details with a verified clinic phone number, address, hours, service area, and emergency referral instructions. Review every claim, credential, testimonial, image, structured-data field, and policy with the appropriate clinical, legal, privacy, and accessibility owners before publishing.
